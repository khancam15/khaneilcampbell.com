Coverage

What the exam
covers.

Six domains, all of which touch ordinary IT operations more than the name suggests.

Domain
Threats and vulnerabilities

Attack types, threat actors, indicators, and the vulnerability classes behind most real incidents.

Domain
Security architecture

Network and system design choices that limit blast radius before anything goes wrong.

Domain
Identity and access management

Authentication, authorisation, MFA, and least privilege — the part that overlaps directly with directory work.

Domain
Security operations

Monitoring, log analysis, incident response, and the evidence handling that makes a response defensible.

Domain
Governance and risk

Policy, risk assessment, and the frameworks that decide what an organisation actually has to do.

Domain
Cryptography basics

Certificates, key management, and transport protection at the level needed to configure them correctly.
