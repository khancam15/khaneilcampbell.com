# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately, never in a public issue. Either:

- Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/khaneilcampbell/khaneilcampbell.com/security/advisories/new), or
- Email **contact@khaneilcampbell.com** with the subject line `Security report`.

Include what you found, where (URL or file) and steps to reproduce. I aim to acknowledge reports within three business days and will keep you updated until the issue is resolved.

## Scope

- The website at https://khaneilcampbell.com
- Its source in this repository, including the GitHub Actions workflows and the `_headers` security headers

Third-party services (GitHub, Cloudflare, Google Fonts) are out of scope; report issues with them to the provider directly.
