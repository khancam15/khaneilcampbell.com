Project 01 · Built

AI product pipeline.

A fully autonomous commerce pipeline. It researches and assembles a brand guide, generates product ideas and listing copy, produces design mockups through Canva automation, publishes to a marketplace over the Etsy REST API, audits its own SEO quality, and emails a daily operational health report.

It runs unattended on a VPS under systemd, cycling through BUILD, DESIGN, and MAINTAIN modes depending on publish limits and configured cooldowns — which means the interesting engineering is not the AI, it is everything around it that stops the AI doing something expensive.

Autonomous does not mean unsupervised. It means the supervision is written down as code.

Flow

Seven phases,
four stages.

Each stage hands a concrete artefact to the next. Nothing publishes until the stage before it has produced something valid.

Phase 1

Brand

CrewAI agents research positioning, visual identity, and SEO direction, then assemble a reusable brand guide.

Phase 2

Generate

Product ideas, titles, tags, descriptions, and template direction generated from the current brand guide.

Phase 3

Design

Canva automation produces mockups and buyer-ready digital template files from the generated direction.

Phase 4–7

Publish

Assets processed, listings validated, published via Etsy, SEO reviewed, and status reported.

Controls

Guardrails,
not good intentions.

The system touches marketplace publishing, OAuth tokens, email, and generated content. These are the controls that keep credentials contained and stop the automation doing something it cannot undo.

Credentials
No committed credentials

API keys and OAuth tokens stay in .env, while .env.example documents the required variables without exposing real values. Runtime output and logs stay out of version control.

Scanning
Pre-commit leak detection

A gitleaks pre-commit hook with redaction enabled catches accidental credentials before they leave the local environment.

OAuth
Token lifecycle handling

Etsy and Canva tokens are acquired through OAuth setup scripts, checked during preflight, and refreshed automatically on expiry rather than hardcoded.

Validation
Checks before publishing

The upload flow validates title length, tag count, price range, asset count, and staged file readiness before attempting a live publish.

Resilience
Controlled API behaviour

Calls retry with exponential backoff on rate limits and server errors, while publish limits and cooldowns prevent burst automation.

Operations
Auditable runtime state

SQLite tables track queue state, published listings, run logs, SEO reviews, and sales — so failures are diagnosable instead of mysterious.
