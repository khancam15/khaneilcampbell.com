Case standard

Four questions, every lab.

What happened, what evidence proves it, how should someone respond, and how does this become repeatable? Anything that cannot answer all four stays marked in progress.

  1. ScenarioRealistic, stated

    A controlled situation with enough realism to produce meaningful telemetry, with assumptions stated up front.

  2. DetectionValidated signal

    Query logic written, the signal validated and the false-positive boundary documented.

  3. TriageFindings, scoped

    Raw events turned into findings with clear severity and scope, and the decision points recorded.

  4. ResponseRepeatable

    The workflow packaged so someone else can repeat and improve it.
