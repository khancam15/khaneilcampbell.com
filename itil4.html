ITIL is the part of the job that decides whether a support function is a queue or a service. Prioritisation, escalation, knowledge management, and change handling all get better when everyone agrees on the same definitions — and worse when they do not.

It shows up in practice in how the labs are documented: the write-up is part of the deliverable, not an optional extra.

Coverage

What the
framework covers.

The concepts that translate most directly into daily support and administration work.

Concept
Service value system

How demand becomes value, and where a support function actually sits inside that path.

Concept
Service value chain

Plan, improve, engage, design, obtain, deliver, support — the activities every request passes through.

Concept
Four dimensions

Organisations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, value streams and processes.

Concept
Guiding principles

Focus on value, start where you are, progress iteratively, collaborate, think holistically, keep it simple, optimise and automate.

Concept
Practices

Incident, service request, change enablement, problem, and knowledge management as defined practices rather than habits.

Concept
Continual improvement

A repeatable improvement model, which is the part that keeps documentation from going stale.
