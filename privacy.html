Privacy and site notice

Nothing to track, by design.

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. Here is exactly what happens when you visit or get in touch.

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who runs this site

khaneilcampbell.com is the personal portfolio of Khaneil Campbell, based in New York. For anything on this page, email contact@khaneilcampbell.com.

What the site collects

Nothing, directly. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or social tracking pixels, no embedded third-party content and no forms. Fonts, images and scripts are all served from this domain, and the page's Content Security Policy blocks it from sending data anywhere.

Hosting and infrastructure

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS and email forwarding. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when it serves a page, to keep the service secure. That data is held by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement, and Cloudflare's handling is covered by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. I do not receive or review visitor logs.

If you email me

Mail to contact@khaneilcampbell.com is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to my personal inbox. I use your name, address and message only to reply. I never sell or share them, and I delete the thread on request.

Links to other sites

LinkedIn, GitHub and other linked sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site. External links open in a new tab and do not pass on which page you came from.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, or ask me to correct or delete it, by emailing the address above. I aim to reply within 30 days.
