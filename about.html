Beyond the work

What drives me.

My interest in technology began on a Windows PC in the early 2000s, where I spent hours playing games and designing geometric shapes in Microsoft Paint. I came of age alongside the social media revolution, from AIM and MySpace to Twitter, Facebook, Snapchat and Instagram. Customizing my MySpace profile meant writing my first lines of code, and that is where technology and creativity first converged for me.

Today, building on my education and five years in enterprise IT, I am developing new expertise in emerging technology, from AI agents to security automation. Having spent years supporting students and faculty at Columbia, I aim to build tools that improve the workflows people rely on every day.

The thread

Fix it twice, write it down. Ten times, automate it.

i.

Evidence first

Reproduce the problem, read the evidence, make the smallest correct change and leave a record so the next person is faster.

ii.

Then automation

Repeated fixes become knowledge articles, then scripts, and now agents with guardrails that are tested before they are trusted.

iii.

Toward leadership

I am growing toward AI solutions engineering and technology leadership, where automation does the volume and people keep the judgment.
